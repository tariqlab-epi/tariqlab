How do cells remember who they are?

We study the molecular machinery that keeps genes active or silent across cell divisions and how those stable identities might be changed.

Our central question

Gene regulation across generations of cells

Cell identities depend on patterns of gene expression passed on as cells divide. We investigate how Polycomb and trithorax proteins preserve that transcriptional memory during development.

The lab at LUMS

Building a home for discovery in Pakistan.

Muhammad Tariq established the Biology program at LUMS and a dedicated epigenetics research group centered on the fruit fly. Since then, the lab has cultivated a robust research and training program. As the first fruit fly research facility in Pakistan, the Fly Room at SBASSE champions experimental work and hands-on teaching, making Drosophila an integral, living component of biology education at LUMS.
